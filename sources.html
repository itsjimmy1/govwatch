Rules we hold ourselves to

  1. Every number carries a source and a date. If we cannot link it to a public record, we do not publish it.
  2. Gaps are listed. Missing data appears as missing, on the dashboard and here.
  3. Counting is not judging. We report that a tax was introduced or that a member voted no. We do not report whether that was wise.
  4. Interpretation is left to the reader. Economists disagree about what a tax burden does to growth. This site does not settle that, and does not yet link the competing analyses either. When it does, they will be linked in pairs.
  5. Corrections are public. Raise an issue and the change and its reason stay in the record.

Known limits

  • State coverage is taxes only. State parliaments, their legislation and their spending are not covered yet, and local councils are not covered at all.
  • Tax per person is what a government collected divided by who lives there. It is not what any individual pays, and it moves with what a state has available to tax.
  • "Acts made per year" counts Acts made, including those since repealed. It measures parliamentary output, not the current size of the statute book. "Acts in force" is the second measure.
  • The tax timeline covers distinct named taxes, 1901 to 2026. Medicare levy rate rises and changes to how an existing tax works are listed but marked, and do not move the count. No other rate, threshold or bracket change is tracked, though all of them move the tax burden.
  • Commonwealth receipts exclude state and local taxation, so the share of GDP shown understates the total tax take across all levels of government.
  • Debt on issue is gross, at face value. It is not net debt and excludes state borrowing.
  • A tax handed from the Commonwealth to the states is recorded as abolished federally. The tax did not disappear; the level of government collecting it changed.
  • Party labels are the party in office at the time and use the name then current.

How to check us

The whole site, including the fetch script and the raw data files, is public at github.com/itsjimmy1/govwatch. Run python3 fetch.py and compare the output to what this site shows.

Every figure here links to the public record it came from. Corrections welcome: open an issue.

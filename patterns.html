What these charts do not show

  • Counting Acts measures output, not effect. A one-page amendment and a tax rewrite both count as one Act.
  • Counting taxes ignores how much each raises. Abolishing a small levy and creating the GST both count as one change.
  • Rates, thresholds and brackets move the tax burden without adding or removing a tax. None of that appears here.
  • A government inherits a Parliament it did not elect and a program it may not control. Attribution by year is rough at the edges.
